On the Course

Highland already has the bones

Hole 10 gold, Highland Road Park, looking back to the tee. DHollier73 / Disc Golf Course Review, November 2019.

BATON ROUGE — People keep pointing at Parc des Familles when they want a Louisiana course that looks like television. Cypress, dedicated fairways, flat. Highland is the other argument: hills, big oaks, a creek, and a gold basket that asks for a real throw. It already has the bones. What it does not have is the care.

Established 1987. First permanent course in Baton Rouge. BREC rebuilt pads, drainage, bridges, and dual purple/gold baskets between 2019 and February 2020. That work is done. BRDGA manages the course. What it still needs is care — not another rebuild story.

Hole 10 gold is the line. Looking back from the basket to the tee you get the hill, the oaks, and the length the BREC map still prints at 726 feet on gold (505 on purple). Creek left. Road long. Longest hole on the property. Give it love — care, routing, respect — not a new capital project. Parc des Familles is not better; Highland has elevation and that gold 10.
