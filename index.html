In the Bag

Schultz’s Worlds plastic

Electron Firm Watt, Silas Schultz 1x World Champion Edition. MVP Pro Shop.

Schultz won Worlds in a playoff. The plastic that followed is stock you can order, with his name on the stamp, from the company that backs him.

Start with the putter. The Electron Firm Watt — Silas Schultz 1x World Champion Edition flies 2 / 5 / −0.5 / 0.5 and lists at $18.95 on the MVP Pro Shop. The stamp marks the 55-foot putt that forced the Worlds playoff. Soft Electron Firm, 9 mm putt-and-approach rim, 165–175 g.

The mid is the Neutron Echo, 2026 World Championship Edition. Streamline. 5 / 5 / −1.5 / 1. Listed at $16.95 on the Pro Shop — sold out at last check. Toboggan DGC stamp by DoubleRam Design. The bag disc from the week he finished it.

The fairway is the Neutron Boost, Silas Schultz World Champion Team Series. Overstable hybrid. 9.5 / 4 / 0 / 2.5. $16.95 on MVP, green and blue still listed. Armory carries a separate LOCK IN Boost Team Series stamp at $19.99 when they have it — different art, same mold family.

A fourth celebration piece sits in the Team Series line: the Prism Plasma Pyro, an overstable Axiom mid at $24.95. Full Worlds shelf: MVP’s 2026 World Champion collection. Check stock at checkout.

Three gadgets that stay in the car

The Bushnell Edge is a disc golf rangefinder with Z-Mode elevation, scan, feet or meters, about 900 feet to the basket. Listed at $199.95.

The GRIPeq BX3 is the backpack people already carry. About 18 discs in the main, Quick Pull putter pocket, lifetime warranty. Discmania sells a JetPack collab of the same frame.

The Visionary 7×7 training net is the home-practice staple. Seven feet by seven. That is the SKU.
